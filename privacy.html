Privacy Policy / Siyaasadda Xogta

Updated 29 September 2026 · Contact: cadeakram72@gmail.com

Who handles your data / Maamulka xogta

Akram operates Akram Library. For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, contact cadeakram72@gmail.com or +252 618 248 533. / Codsiyada xogtaada kala xiriir email-ka ama lambarka kor ku qoran.

Information used / Xogta la isticmaalo

We use account identifiers and email, Google profile information when you choose Google sign-in, purchased book and order records, payment references/status, reviews, support/refund requests and account-linked reading positions. The website keeps sign-in sessions, language, appearance, favorites, notes and some reading information in browser storage. / Waxaan isticmaalnaa xogta akoonka, dalabyada, tixraaca lacagta, faallooyinka iyo halka akhrisku marayo; dejimo qaar waxay ku kaydsan yihiin browser-ka.

Why / Sababta

Data is used to authenticate you, grant the correct book access, prevent duplicate purchases, restore reading position, provide support and maintain transaction records. Optional newsletter signup is separate from purchase receipts. We do not sell your personal data. / Xogtu waxay taageertaa gelitaanka, furitaanka buugga saxda ah, xusuusinta akhriska iyo caawinta.

Service providers / Adeeg-bixiyeyaasha

GitHub Pages serves this website; Supabase handles authentication, database and book storage. Google processes sign-in if chosen. An activated payment provider processes payments on its own secure page. An activated email provider sends purchase receipts. External fonts/CDN resources may receive your IP address when loaded. Some providers may process data outside your country. / Adeeg-bixiyeyaashu waxay martigeliyaan website-ka, akoonnada, buugaagta, bixinta iyo email-ka; xog qaar dal kale ayaa lagu farsamayn karaa.

Payment information / Xogta lacagta

Akram Library does not request your mobile-money PIN or store full card numbers/CVV. Enter payment credentials only on the payment provider’s own approved interface. We retain transaction identifiers and results required for access and reconciliation. / PIN-kaaga iyo CVV-ga ha noo soo dirin; waxaan kaydinaa tixraaca iyo natiijada lacagta.

Retention and your choices / Kaydinta iyo doorashada

Contact us to request a copy, correction or deletion of your information and to unsubscribe from optional updates. Clearing browser data removes local preferences; server-held purchases remain linked to your account. Transaction records may need to be retained for legitimate accounting, dispute or legal requirements. / Waxaad codsan kartaa nuqul, sixid ama tirtirid xogtaada. Diiwaanno lacag-bixin ayaa loo hayn karaa xisaab ama waajibaad sharci.

Safety / Ilaalinta

Access is restricted by account and administrative permissions. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Report suspected misuse promptly. If you believe a child supplied personal data without appropriate permission, contact us. / Xogta waxaa lagu xaddidayaa oggolaanshaha akoonka. Haddii aad aragto isticmaal khaldan nala soo xiriir.
